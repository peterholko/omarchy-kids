## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent character sprite atlas for Pawberry Pet Hotel, a cozy arithmetic game for an 11-year-old girl. Create ONE square 2048x2048 PNG sprite sheet containing exactly 20 different cute full-body animal pets, aligned in a strict 5-column by 4-row grid of equal 409.6px square cells. Real transparent alpha background; no baked checkerboard, backdrop, floor, shared shadow, boxes or grid lines. Each animal fits completely within its own cell with generous clear margins of at least 12% on every side, including ears, wings and tail; nothing crosses cell boundaries. Animals have comparable visual size, are centered, sitting or standing front-facing, with their heads in the upper half of their cell. Cohesive polished storybook illustration, soft painted fur and feather details, big gentle dark eyes, blush cheeks, warm pastel colors, adorable but suited to a tween; natural recognizable animal anatomy, two eyes each. Do not dress the animals: accessories will be drawn by the game separately. Exactly this reading order from left to right and top to bottom: Row 1: cream-and-caramel hamster; ginger-and-white guinea pig; soft grey chinchilla with large round ears; tiny brown hedgehog; cream ferret with a brown eye mask. Row 2: fluffy red panda with a ringed tail; orange fox with white chest and black ear tips; cocoa brown otter with little paws; plump pale grey seal pup with flippers; small mint green turtle with a rounded shell. Row 3: golden orange parrot; pale yellow cockatiel with crest and rosy cheek patches; fluffy yellow duckling; baby blue-grey penguin with white belly; friendly tan capybara. Row 4: fluffy cream alpaca with a long neck; tiny peach pink piglet; caramel fawn with white spots and delicate ears; silver-grey raccoon with a ringed tail and dark mask; pink axolotl with coral external gills. Exactly 20 animals, one per cell, all different. No humans, no clothing, no lettering, no numbers, no objects, no logos, no existing franchise characters, no watermark. Ensure the entire 5 by 4 grid and every single animal are visible and unclipped.
